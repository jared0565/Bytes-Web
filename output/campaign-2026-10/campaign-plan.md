# BytesFX: Turn visits into enquiries

Proposed campaign · 18 October 2026 · Not published

## Objective and positioning

Help UK service businesses turn existing website attention into relevant sales conversations. BytesFX combines conversion-focused websites, custom applications and AI chatbots with lead capture and follow-up. The campaign sells a useful next step rather than promising a financial result.

Assumed initial audience: UK founders, owner-led service businesses and small sales/operations teams. This is a starting hypothesis, not a verified customer segment. Keep agency enquiries and chatbot signups as separate funnels.

**Agency offer:** request a free consultation for a website, app or chatbot project. Destination: `https://web.bytesfx.com/#contact`. Say “request”, not “book instantly”: the current destination is a contact form, not a calendar.

**Product offer:** a 7-day free trial of AI chatbot replies and AI email follow-ups. No card needed. Destination: `https://web.bytesfx.com/onboarding/`. Trial allowances: 1 website, 10 leads/day, 50 total contacts; BytesFX badge included. After trial, Free uses scripted chat. Paid AI plans start at US$29/month, with upgrades arranged manually through the team. AI usage and chat limits also apply; the trial is not the full Pro plan.

## Conversion measurement

Track the two funnels separately:

- Consultation: campaign visits → successful form submissions → qualified enquiries → held consultations → won projects.
- Product: campaign visits → verified signups → installed widget on an allowed domain → first relevant lead → paid upgrade confirmed by an admin.

Define a qualified agency enquiry as an identifiable prospect with a relevant service need and a credible route to a project; record fit, timeline and next step. Define product activation as a verified signup plus widget installation on the customer's site, not merely opening onboarding.

Every creative has a distinct `utm_campaign`. The current cookieless beacon records `utm_source` and `utm_campaign` on entry, but does not record `utm_content` or `utm_medium`. These latter tags are included for compatibility, not presented as existing dashboard capabilities. Current tracking removes UTM tags from the address bar. Cross-page/lead-level campaign attribution has not been verified and must not be assumed. The contact form emits a `generate_lead` event, but that alone does not prove platform conversion attribution. DNT/GPC and automation exclusions also mean page counts are partial.

For the organic pilot, record post ID, publication date, channel, reach, outbound clicks, relevant comments, attributable enquiries, qualification and outcome in a simple weekly sheet. If campaign-to-lead linkage is unavailable, label it unknown; use an optional “where did you hear about us?” question during the sales conversation. Do not infer conversion rates by joining incompatible anonymous visit and lead totals.

Primary metrics: qualified consultation requests and activated trials, then held consultations and manually confirmed paid upgrades. Secondary diagnostics: outbound click-through rate, signup completion and first-lead rate. Use cost per qualified enquiry and cost per activated trial only when spending and attribution are actually available. No numeric performance lift is promised.

## Testing and optional paid expansion

Start with the organic pilot. There is no approved media budget or paid launch in this pack. If a small budget is later approved, test one offer on one platform at a time; spreading a small budget across six ads will make interpretation difficult.

Proposed hypotheses:

1. **Website framing:** “Turn visits into enquiries” versus “Make your next website work harder.” Keep audience, CTA, art and destination constant.
2. **Trial framing:** after-hours availability versus less manual follow-up. Keep offer, limits and destination constant.
3. **Destination:** direct onboarding for the product offer versus the mixed-offer homepage. Only run this once source-to-activation tracking is verified.

Compare variants within an offer and channel; do not treat different LinkedIn/Facebook audiences as a controlled A/B test. Review after a complete business week; avoid declaring a winner from a handful of clicks. If conversion volume is too low, record the result as inconclusive and use qualified conversations to guide the next test. Do not scale on click-through rate alone. Set an allowable acquisition cost from gross margin, expected retention and sales capacity before paid launch; set the spend cap and stop rule with the budget owner.

For an eventual paid audience, begin with relevant UK business decision-makers; confirm available job roles, geography and targeting in the current ad interfaces. Do not assume job-title filters are available equally on both platforms. Retarget only when appropriate consent, privacy disclosures and a sufficient eligible audience are in place. No tracking pixel or customer list has been installed or uploaded for this work.

## Pre-publication checks

- Check the live contact form and onboarding journey, including email verification and widget installation, using an authorised test process. Local website forms use live APIs and can send real emails.
- Confirm live trial terms, currency, pricing, limits and post-trial entitlement behaviour. The knowledge base flags an unresolved post-trial AI email entitlement gap; the campaign describes the intended offer, not a guarantee of backend enforcement. Resolve or clarify that gap before promoting paid upgrades at scale.
- Confirm that human review is enabled for proposals; auto-approval settings may change behaviour. Keep illustrations labelled and never use invented customer messages as testimonials.
- Confirm the manual paid-upgrade process has an owner and a reliable response path. No payment checkout is claimed.
- Verify successful conversion tracking and source handling before using a conversion-optimised paid objective. Current pageview tracking alone is insufficient.
- Validate crops, readable trial notes, link previews and alt text in each composer. Put the tracked URL in the post body; the URL printed in the image is for recognition.
- Keep verified customer evidence separate from design examples. Homepage counters claiming 150+ projects, 98% satisfaction and 50% ROI are excluded because substantiation was not found.
