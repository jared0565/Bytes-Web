# Image 03 readability revision — LinkedIn and Facebook

The original full amber background made the composition visually loud. Dark green emphasis had 3.98:1 contrast on amber; although sufficient for large text, the colour hierarchy and small supporting labels were difficult to scan at feed size. Workflow labels were 20px (LinkedIn), stage numbers 13px and trial notes 17–18px in the native exports.

Both versions now use petrol #071216, off-white #f4f7f5, mint #57e3bf and an amber #ffb547 CTA. Workflow panels use #15312f, with a pale mint #d9f6eb final review panel. Supporting copy and trial terms use #d3e1dc.

| Text role | Contrast ratio |
|---|---:|
| Off-white headline / petrol | 17.59:1 |
| Mint emphasis / petrol | 11.87:1 |
| Supporting copy and trial terms / petrol | 14.07:1 |
| Workflow labels / dark teal panels | 12.87:1 |
| Stage numbers / dark teal panels | 9.11:1 |
| Final review number / pale mint panel | 7.95:1 |
| Petrol CTA text / amber button | 10.80:1 |

Ratios calculated from the solid sRGB foreground/background colours using relative luminance. These quantify palette contrast, not overall accessibility certification of a raster image.

Workflow labels increased to 24px on LinkedIn and 28px on Facebook; stage numbers to 18px; trial terms to 22px on both. The Facebook workflow was moved upward to preserve spacing. Export dimensions remain 1200 × 1200 and 1080 × 1350 respectively.

Both final PNGs were visually inspected after export. Measured element bounds confirm no overlap between workflow, trial terms and CTA. Editable source, image files and the downloadable pack were updated together.
