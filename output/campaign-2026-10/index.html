THE CONVERSION LOGIC

Two offers. Clear destinations.

01 / WEBSITE & APPS

Start with the business goal.

For owners planning a website, refresh or custom app. Lead with a clearer path to enquiry. Send people to the consultation form at /#contact.

02 / CHATBOT TRIAL

Make the first step tangible.

For businesses with a website already. Demonstrate questions, lead capture and follow-up. Send people directly to /onboarding/, with trial limits visible.

03 / MEASURE THE OUTCOME

Count conversations that progress.

Consultation track: qualified enquiries and held calls. Trial track: verified signups, installed widgets and qualified leads. Use clicks as a diagnostic, not the success metric.

Source-grounded, ready for review. Copy is based on README.md, docs/knowledge-base.md, plans.json and the homepage/onboarding source. Live-site retrieval failed on 18 October 2026. Conceptual chat and workflow visuals are illustrations. No customer results or ROI guarantees are asserted. Paid upgrades currently go through the team.
