Data Deletion

Instructions for removing your data from BytesAI

If you signed in to BytesAI using Facebook, Google, or LinkedIn, you can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

How to delete your data

  1. Send an email to privacy@bytsfx.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your BytesAI account
  3. We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture)
  • Social login association (Google, Facebook, or LinkedIn)
  • All leads, contacts, and campaign data linked to your account
  • Chat logs and conversation history
  • Chatbot configuration and settings

Facebook-specific deletion

If you connected your Facebook account and want to remove BytesAI's access, you can also revoke access directly through Facebook:

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  2. Find BytesAI CRM and click Remove
  3. This revokes Facebook's data sharing with BytesAI immediately

To also delete any data already stored on our servers, send us an email using the button below.

✉ Request Data Deletion
