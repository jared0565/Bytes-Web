Data Deletion

Instructions for removing your data from BytesFX

Under data protection laws, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data. We honor all deletion requests within 30 days.

How to delete your data

  1. Send an email to contact@bytesfx.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your inquiry or account
  3. We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days

What gets deleted

  • Your contact information (name, email, phone)
  • Any project details or messages you submitted
  • Lead information stored through our website
  • Chat conversation history
  • Any other personal data you provided to us

Cookie data

To delete cookies stored on your device, you can:

  1. Clear your browser cookies through your browser settings
  2. Or use our cookie consent banner on your next visit to update preferences
