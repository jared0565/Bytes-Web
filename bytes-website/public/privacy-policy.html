Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

BytesAI ("we", "our", or "us") operates a CRM and lead generation platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account registration details (name, email address)
  • Authentication data when you sign in via Google, Facebook, or LinkedIn
  • Business information entered during onboarding (company name, chatbot configuration)
  • Lead and contact data you add to the platform
  • Usage data and chat logs generated through embedded chatbot widgets

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our CRM services
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Generate AI-powered email sequences and campaign content
  • Send transactional and service-related communications
  • Monitor platform performance and prevent abuse

3. Social Login (OAuth)

When you choose to sign in using Google, Facebook, or LinkedIn, we receive basic profile information from those providers — typically your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not receive or store your social media passwords. The information received is used solely for account authentication and identification.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service providers — infrastructure and hosting providers (Cloudflare) necessary to operate the platform
  • AI providers — anonymised content may be processed by Google Gemini to generate email sequences
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), HTTP-only session cookies, and rate limiting. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Cookies

We use session cookies solely for authentication purposes. These cookies are HTTP-only and are not accessible to JavaScript. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
