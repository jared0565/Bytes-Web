    This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how BytesFX uses cookies on our website, and your choices regarding cookies.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience. Cookies can be "persistent" (remain on your device until deleted) or "session" (deleted when you close your browser).

    2. How We Use Cookies

    BytesFX uses cookies for the following purposes:

    Cookie Type Purpose Duration
    Essential Cookies Required for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas. Session / Persistent
    Analytics Cookies Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. Persistent (up to 2 years)
    Functional Cookies Enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as live chat support. Persistent (up to 1 year)
    Cookie Consent Remembers your cookie preferences so you don't see the consent banner on each visit. Persistent (1 year)

    3. Cookies We Use

    Essential Cookies

    • Session cookies — Maintain your session as you navigate our website
    • Security cookies — Help us detect suspicious activity and protect your data

    Analytics Cookies

    We use analytics tools that may set cookies to help us understand website traffic and usage patterns. These tools collect information anonymously and help us improve our website.

    Chatbot Cookies

    If you use our AI chatbot, temporary cookies may be set to maintain your conversation session. These are essential for the chatbot to function properly.

    4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

    When you first visit our website, a cookie banner lets you choose, with equal ease:

    • Accept all — allow analytics and marketing cookies
    • Reject all — keep only the necessary cookies
    • Customise — switch analytics and marketing on or off individually

    Analytics and marketing tools stay in a restricted, cookieless mode until you opt in. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, marketing cookies are off by default. We ask again after 6 months.

    You can change your choice at any time:

    • Open cookie settings (also linked in every page footer)
    • Clear your browser cookies and site data

    5. Browser Settings

    Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

    • View what cookies are stored on your device
    • Delete all or specific cookies
    • Block all or certain types of cookies
    • Set preferences for certain websites

    Here are links to cookie management instructions for popular browsers:

    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari

    6. Third-Party Cookies

    Some third-party services we use may set cookies on your device. These include:

    • Analytics providers — For website analytics
    • CDN providers — For content delivery

    We do not have control over these third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective third-party policies for more information.

    7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

    If you disable non-essential cookies, some features of our website may not work properly, including:

    • Personalized content
    • Chatbot functionality
    • Analytics tracking (which helps us improve our website)

    Essential cookies are required for the website to function and cannot be disabled.

    We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
